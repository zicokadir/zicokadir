01 / About the show

No filter.
All hoop dreams.

The Foul And One is Kenya's loudest podcast about the game we love — raw, humorous and unapologetically passionate takes on the KNBL, school leagues and streetball across the country.

From buzzer-beaters at the dumpsite courts to playoff drama between KPA Dockers and Dumas Basketball, we break it all down — then we say what everyone's thinking but nobody dares to say. Part analysis, part comedy, fully Kenyan.

Every week the crew hits the mic, guests bring the heat, and the comments section brings the war. Clips go viral, fans get roasted, and our beloved Kenyan basketballers finally get the spotlight they deserve.
